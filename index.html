Rehan Dominators Mahuawan (RDM) Cricket Team

Rehan Dominators Mahuawan, known as RDM or RDM Mahuawan, is an official competitive cricket club from Mahuawan, Gopalganj, Bihar. Follow our 17-player roster, match fixtures, live scorecards, tournament standings, and team honours.
